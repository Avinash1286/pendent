The A03 capsule is the center of the story: glossy black full-face record paddle, satin silver frame, and fine necklace chain. Use actual Blender geometry, broad photographic lighting, and breathing room. Start with the low-angle profile before revealing the name. Warm ivory headline text stays crisp against graphite; apricot is reserved for a recording moment and a short line. Never use multicolor gradients, fake review stars, invented benchmarks, or crowded feature cards.

At 1920×1080 use 77–158 px display type, 28–43 px supporting type, and 20–23 px labels. Keep type within a 120 px safe area. Product photographs can run past the image boundary intentionally while text remains entirely legible. The closing chain fades before reaching the tagline.

Motion is slow, deliberate, and finite: 2.5–5.5% photographic reframes with continuous movement. Entrance type resolves with cubic ease-out. The final lockup is fully resolved by 31.35 seconds and holds until 36 seconds. No endless ambient loops, bounce, or gratuitous transitions.

The headline tracking reveal adapts the installed Hyperframes `tracking-in` primitive. The final wordmark settlement and line drawing adapt the installed `titlecard-lockup` primitive. Composition otherwise follows the project's own visual hierarchy.

# Asset inventory

No website was captured: this film is based on the user's product brief and the actual Blender enclosure designed in the same project.

- `assets/hero-transparent.png`: transparent Blender beauty render of the AURA A03 capsule. Glossy black full-face recording paddle, satin silver frame, tiny white indicator, and fine necklace chain. The actual model follows the user's selected capsule reference.
- `assets/hero-macro.png`, `assets/hero-close.png`: separate, identical source plates for the macro and closing shots; distinct filenames keep Hyperframes media discovery unambiguous.
- `assets/detail.png`: Blender macro of the physical microphone disconnect on the right side of the silver frame. Actual product geometry, not stock photography.
- `assets/profile.png`: low-angle Blender profile showing the capsule's silhouette, full black face, silver edge, fine seam, and attachment bail.
- `assets/aura-score.wav`: original 36-second stereo ambient music synthesized locally with `scripts/score.mjs`. No third-party music samples, voices, or paid generators.
- `assets/fonts/Manrope.ttf`: Manrope variable font from the official Google Fonts repository; SIL Open Font License bundled as `assets/fonts/OFL.txt`.
- `assets/vendor/gsap.min.js`: local copy of GSAP3.14.2 installed through npm for network-independent rendering.

The film's notes surface is an authored interface concept, labeled as such on screen, with the phone-and-cloud architecture identified. It does not claim a production-ready companion service or validated hardware performance.

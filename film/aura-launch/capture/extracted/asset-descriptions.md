# Asset inventory

No website was captured: this film is based on the user's product brief and the actual Blender enclosure designed in the same project.

- `assets/hero-transparent.png`: final transparent Blender beauty render of AURA. A satin light titanium pebble form with tactile circular recording button, tiny indicator, necklace attachment and cord. Staged after enclosure validation.
- `assets/hero-macro.png`, `assets/hero-close.png`: separate, identical source plates for the macro and closing shots; distinct filenames keep Hyperframes media discovery unambiguous.
- `assets/detail.png`: Blender studio macro of the physical microphone disconnect slider. Actual product geometry, not stock photography.
- `assets/aura-score.wav`: original 36-second stereo ambient music synthesized locally with `scripts/score.mjs`. No third-party music samples, voices, or paid generators.
- `assets/fonts/Manrope.ttf`: Manrope variable font from the official Google Fonts repository; SIL Open Font License bundled as `assets/fonts/OFL.txt`.
- `assets/vendor/gsap.min.js`: local copy of GSAP3.14.2 installed through npm for network-independent rendering.

The film's notes surface is an authored interface concept, labeled as such on screen. It does not claim an implemented companion application.

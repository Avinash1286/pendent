## Intent

A premium launch film for AURA, a necklace-sized, battery-powered AI note-taking pendant concept. Original art direction takes inspiration from the restraint, lighting, and pacing of high-end consumer product films. The user explicitly delegates all decisions and requests a finished video using Hyperframes.

## Assets

- ../../enclosure/renders/hero-transparent.png — actual Blender product beauty render, staged locally before final render.
- ../../enclosure/renders/hero.png — actual Blender studio environment.
- ../../enclosure/renders/detail.png — hardware detail, staged locally before final render.
- assets/aura-score.wav — original locally synthesized music composed specifically for this film.

## Customizations

- 36 seconds, 1920×1080, 30 fps, MP4/H.264 with AAC stereo audio.
- Seekable GSAP motion driven entirely by Hyperframes.
- No voiceover: the product, measured typography, and original score lead the film.
- Graphite, warm ivory, satin light titanium, and a small apricot accent.

## Notes

All shown features are design intent for a development concept. No shipping date, live checkout, certification, measured battery life, or demonstrated AI performance is claimed. The phone notes screen is an explicitly labeled interface concept. No user decision or approval checkpoint is required: the user expressly asked the agent to choose and finish the entire deliverable.

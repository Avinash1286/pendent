## Findings requiring disposition

1. **Radio antenna keepout: corrected in source during review.** The original full-width keepout started at Y=12.7 mm. U1 is centered at Y=8 mm and its antenna edge is at Y=15.75 mm, leaving only 3.05 mm of depth. Raytac specifies 3.8 mm. The revised TSX now uses a 5.05 mm-high, four-layer keepout centered at Y=14.475 mm, placing its boundary at **Y=11.95 mm**. Confirm the final exported rule area and copper against this boundary. [Raytac manufacturer approval sheet, Version L, p.11; copy hosted by Espruino](https://www.espruino.com/datasheets/MDBT50Q-1M.pdf).

2. **Unused U1 VBUS, pad 32: corrected in source during review.** Originally NC, pad 32 is now mapped to GND and excluded from the generated NC list. This follows the USB-disabled reference, which grounds VBUS while leaving D+/D− open. Check regenerated netlist/board continuity. [Raytac Version L, p.46](https://www.espruino.com/datasheets/MDBT50Q-1M.pdf), [Nordic's matching integration recommendation](https://devzone.nordicsemi.com/f/nordic-q-a/75473/nrf52840-schematic-questions/311313).

3. **Resolve cell temperature compatibility before selecting or charging a cell.** The direct 10 kΩ, B25/85=3435 K NTC is the specified charger sensor type, but it does not implement a 45°C cutoff. Using the stated beta approximation, `Rhot = 0.115 V / 38 µA = 3026 Ω` gives **59.5°C nominal**. IC threshold/current corners alone give approximately **55.6–63.7°C**, before thermistor tolerance and thermal lag; nominal cold entry is approximately 1.7°C. The pack remains unspecified. Match its permitted charge-temperature range using a reviewed thermistor network or another qualified hardware limit. The existing MCU receives charger fault status, not a continuous battery-temperature measurement. [BQ25185 datasheet, electrical characteristics and §6.3.9](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq25185.pdf). These temperatures are calculations, not measurements.

4. **Move critical bypass capacitors close to their associated IC pins before final routing.** The placement override puts U3 at (−4.3,−3.8) and its SYS capacitor C9 at (−1.3,−13.55), roughly 10.2 mm apart; C8 is also near the battery pads rather than U3. U5 and its sole bypass C11 are roughly 9.9 mm apart. These center distances are a screening result, not measured trace inductance. Place local capacitance with short supply/ground loops; then inspect the actual routes. U3 specifically calls for C_SYS close to SYS/GND, and U5 requires local supply bypassing. [BQ25185 pin table/layout guidance](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq25185.pdf), [MAX17048 datasheet, pin descriptions and layout guidance](https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/max17048-max17049.pdf).

5. **Resolve the microphone bypass dielectric choice.** C3/C4 are generic X7R parts. The retrieved Knowles manufacturer Rev.A sheet advises against Class 2 dielectrics near the microphones; X7R is Class 2. Select an acoustic-compatible capacitor and placement, or obtain current manufacturer guidance justifying the choice. This is an acoustic-performance risk, not a demonstrated electrical failure. [Knowles SPH0641LU4H-1 Rev.A, p.8; distributor-hosted manufacturer copy](https://xonstorage.z8.web.core.windows.net/pdf/knowles_sph0641lu4h1_apr22_xonlink.pdf). The current Rev.B manufacturer link was unavailable, so this point needs revision confirmation.

## Limits and exact follow-up checks

- **Privacy transitions are unproven.** U8 output enables are tied low; its Ioff guarantee applies at VCC=0 V, not every point of a power ramp. Scope MIC_VDD, microphone clock/data and MCU data during slider bounce, recording start/stop, reset and brownout, including a driven MCU clock while mechanically OFF. Prove absence of usable audio after cutoff. Verify the DPDT contact sequence and actuator orientation on the actual switch. An open-circuit LED can fail dark while microphones remain powered; this topology is an indicator, not an LED-failure interlock.
- **NAND verification is incomplete.** The authored single-SPI wiring and firmware's explicit ECC/bad-block/power-loss requirements are coherent, but the full current Winbond datasheet was not retrieved. No pin-to-package, exposed-pad or timing signoff is claimed for U2. Obtain the exact ZE package drawing and supply/programming limits from [Winbond's documentation index](https://www.winbond.com/hq/support/documentation/?__locale=en&pno=W25N01GV). Interrupt power during real NAND program/erase and metadata commits before relying on note recovery.
- **Low-battery behavior needs measurements.** Define a voltage floor that preserves V3 and flash write margin under haptic and radio peaks. Battery charge current is 0.30C at the 250 mAh target. The source was updated from 200 mAh during review; synchronize generated BOM, envelope and endurance assumptions without implying a qualified cell.
- **No physical-design pass was performed here.** At review time, `routed-validation.json` dated 2026-09-07T18:36:52Z reported one autorouting error and 199 unconnected-port findings. A later routing result supersedes that snapshot but requires its own inspection. Logical invariants do not establish electrical pin types, net continuity, return paths or thermal performance. No clean ERC or DRC is claimed.

The antenna/VBUS corrections and NTC concern were sent directly to the hardware author during review. Before fabrication, record their disposition, review exact component land patterns and effective capacitances, complete electrical-rule and physical connectivity checks, and obtain qualified battery/RF engineering review followed by the listed bench tests.

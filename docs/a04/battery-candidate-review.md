Reviewed 2026-09-09. No order, supplier message, CAD change or battery selection was made. The current schematic contract is unchanged.

**LiPol Battery Co. LP402024 with PCM is the strongest documented candidate, but it is not approved for purchase or wear.** Its dated pack drawing covers the proposed electrical envelope and increases required pack depth to 4.3 mm maximum. The remaining hold is now specific: obtain a current, mutually consistent pack specification for that exact assembly, including PCM behavior and attached sensor. A generic cell model number or a protection trip threshold is insufficient.

## Why the closest candidate still has a hold

LiPol's numerical charge, current and dimensional lines are useful design inputs. Its PDF also lists a 4.20 V operating-range upper limit alongside the explicit 4.2 V +/-50 mV charge line, and PCM recovery entries need confirmation. The [same supplier's product page](https://www.lipobatteries.net/hot-selling-lipo-battery/standard-lipo-battery-lp402024-165mah/) contains inconsistent 10/50 mm wire descriptions and 400/800 mA charging boilerplate. Use none of those larger currents. The specific dated drawing is the provisional reference; it does not prove the currently delivered assembly or erase conflicting documentation.

The exact remaining supplier question is: **Will the supplied LP402024 PCM assembly conform to FD_2024_70 index3 or a named current revision, including a 25 x 20.5 x 4.3 mm maximum finished body, continuous 165 mA discharge, and permitted CC/CV charger regulation up to 4.221 V over the approved charge-temperature range? Provide corrected PCM detection/release values, charging overshoot allowance, current traceable transport documentation and the exact cell/PCM/wire/insulation/NTC assembly drawing.** This question is recorded, not sent.

Do not substitute the same-looking **EEMB LP402024-PCM-LD**: its manufacturer document ZJQM-RD-SPC-A02226, 2016-08-02, limits charging to **4.200 +/-0.020 V**, so AURA's 4.221 V corner exceeds it by 1 mV. The cell's 330 mA rating also does not describe the pack: the PCM table permits only **100 mA continuous** and 300 mA for 3 seconds. Its finished pack is 26 x 20.5 x 4.3 mm maximum. This is a concrete example of why manufacturer and full pack suffix must travel with the model number. [EEMB-authored specification, distributor mirror, pp.3,7-8](https://macrogroup.ru/upload/iblock/e69/2jmx02dtp5szkd1q9hovh77fmnbxfzqy/LP402024.pdf).

## Mechanical and electrical consequence if LiPol is confirmed

Retain the 26 x 21 mm XY allocation as a proposed pocket: the 25 x 20.5 mm body leaves only 1.0 x 0.5 mm total clearance before carrier/insulation, so actual assembly tolerances still matter. Increase the pack depth allocation from 3.3 to **4.3 mm**, preserving the separate 0.4 mm growth budget, lead relief and cell-edge sensor pocket. With all other provisional layers unchanged, body depth becomes approximately **11.8 mm with the actual 1.6 mm PCB**, or **11.0 mm if a 0.8 mm PCB is actually authored**. These are arithmetic stack consequences, not a fit claim. The mechanical owner must recheck the cell corners, dock carrier, right-side NTC pocket and acoustic paths before adopting them.

Keep BQ25186 default/reset tolerance in the comparison: `4.2 x 1.005 = 4.221 V`. Normal 4.17 V configuration cannot be used to waive reset behavior. Keep the conservative R22 =3.3 kOhm thermal candidate and verify the sensor's assembled resistance and lag. Do not infer that the pack's multiampere PCM overcurrent threshold enforces a 165 mA operating limit. Measure all simultaneous loads and startup transients before reducing the 160 mA design budget or claiming runtime.

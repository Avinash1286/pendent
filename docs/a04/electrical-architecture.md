# AURA A04 electrical architecture

Reviewed 2026-09-09. This is the electrical design input for the fresh circular A04 KiCad MCP project. It is **not a fabrication, battery, or first-wear approval**. Root owns every CAD mutation. No A03 PCB, placement, route or schematic file was copied into A04 by this review.

## Selected architecture

Retain **Raytac MDBT50Q-1MV2 / nRF52840**, two opposite-edge PDM microphones, hardware microphone disconnect, a dedicated microphone-power light, local audio storage, BLE transfer, fuel gauge, a face button and tactile feedback. Add separate **Macronix MX25R3235FM1IL0 32 Mbit NOR** for signed update staging, physical recovery reset, and independently controlled perimeter illumination. Use **BQ25186DLHR** for controlled charging and power path, with a default-inactive external charge-permission gate. Transcription, playback and AI actions use the companion/host; this circuit does not contain a speaker, Wi-Fi radio or an on-device LLM.

The reference's polished appearance requires an RF-transparent prototype body/finish around the antenna. Conductive plating, the chain, magnets, screws and battery cannot be treated as decorative details inside the antenna exclusion volume. The 40 mm body and 10 mm thickness remain design aspirations. Current MCP board thickness is 1.6 mm; the mechanical team's otherwise unchanged stack is approximately **10.8 mm**. A 0.8 mm target must actually be authored and verified before claiming the thinner result. See [mechanical contract](industrial-design.md).

[Pin definitions](pin-definitions.json) hold all 61 module pins and all 11 charger pads with sources and types. [Schematic contract](schematic-contract.json) assigns **72 PCB references / 239 connected pin endpoints**; 29 module pins are used and 32 intentionally unused. Its [generator](build-electrical-contract.py) writes documentation JSON only, using previous reviewed pin assignments for unchanged parts. From the repository root run `python docs/a04/build-electrical-contract.py`, then `python docs/a04/validate-electrical-contract.py`; the Python standard library is sufficient. The scripts resolve their data paths independently of the current working directory. [Validation evidence](electrical-validation.json) binds both input hashes and reproduces the conditional thermal arithmetic. New symbol/footprint checks, ERC and netlist parity remain required after MCP capture.

## Why the module stays

The nRF52840 has 1 MiB flash, 256 KiB RAM and an M4F core. Omi's same-family Opus use justifies an AURA qualification experiment, not a measured AURA performance or runtime claim. Qualify encoding, BLE scheduling, buffer headroom and local persistence together. Keep calibrated LFRC initially; do not claim a populated 32.768 kHz crystal. The module contains the 32 MHz crystal and Reg1 inductor. [Raytac Version L, pp.4-5,17,40-41](https://www.raytac.com/tw/download/index.php?index_id=24), [reviewed manufacturer PDF mirror](https://www.espruino.com/datasheets/MDBT50Q-1M.pdf), [software acceptance contract](software-contract.md).

The smaller Insight SiP **ISP1807-LR** was rejected for this revision. Its 8 x 8 x 1 mm nominal body uses a double-row 78-contact LGA, 0.65 mm pitch, isolated mechanical lands and an 18 mm-wide RF exclusion. Its inner-pad escape and via treatment add manufacturing risk. A smaller package alone does not simplify this personal prototype. [Manufacturer product and datasheet](https://www.insightsip.com/products/bluetooth-le-modules/isp1807).

## Battery and charging: what is actually selected

**No battery is approved yet.** The [dated three-pack comparison](battery-candidate-review.md) identifies LiPol LP402024 PCM as the strongest conditional candidate, with a 25 x 20.5 x 4.3 mm maximum body and an explicit charge-tolerance line encompassing 4.221 V. Conflicting PCM/document entries and current assembly control keep it on hold. No current pocket or wiring contract has been silently changed. The controlled target is a protected single-cell pack no larger than26 x21 x3.3 mm including PCM, tabs, insulation and routing; the thermistor integration must be included or receive a separate proven pocket. Capacity is not committed. The architecture reserves160 mA continuous discharge capability until peak-load tests establish a lower defensible requirement. This is a procurement requirement, not a measured consumption or an enforced current limit.

FPBattery/DNK302020120 mAh is a concrete research candidate:22+/-1 mm protected length, nominal20 x3 mm cross-section, maximum charge60 mA, discharge120 mA. Its preliminary sheet does not establish maximum width/thickness, attached sensor, allowed charger-voltage tolerance or160 mA system budget. Therefore it is **not selected for wear or purchase**. A03's DNK302025150 mAh has28 mm maximum full length and does not satisfy the new26 mm limit. Both expose why bare cell model numbers cannot define the pack pocket. [302020 preliminary sheet](https://www.fpbattery.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/fpbattery-302020-3.7V-120mAh-Lithium-Polymer-Battery-Specification.pdf), [302025 sheet](https://www.fpbattery.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/fpbattery-302025-3.7V-150mAh-Lithium-Polymer-Battery-Specification.pdf).

Set BQ25186 normal charge target40 mA and4.17 V, with input limit100 mA and all thermal protection enabled. Its default/reset voltage is4.20 V; with0.5% regulation tolerance the pack must accept **4.221 V**. Software4.17 V only gives4.19085 V in that configured state. It does not cover reset. Default fast-charge current is10 mA; default watchdog begins after the first I2C transaction and, after160 s without a transaction, resets all read/write registers without necessarily resetting the MCU. Therefore a stuck-high permission GPIO can coexist with default4.20 V charging. Hardware reset also restores defaults. Keep the external `/CE` pullup to VSYS and CHARGE_ALLOW pulldown. Read back configuration before asserting permission, and deassert on fault or unexpected register values. An approved pack tolerance or separately proven hardware overvoltage cutoff remains mandatory. [BQ25186 pp.3,6,20,23,32-40](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq25186.pdf).

The BQ25186's SYS default4.5 V goes through the3 V regulators; do not connect it directly to the radio or new motor. External input limiting is still needed because the charger default input limit is much greater than100 mA. The battery PCM overcurrent threshold must be reviewed separately; a several-ampere protection threshold does not enforce a small cell's permissible discharge current.

## Independent hot gate and honest temperature bounds

Select conservative **R22 = 3.30 kOhm, 1%, RC0402FR-073K3L**, between the cell NTC and BAT_TS. BAT_TS feeds BQ pin 6 and high-impedance TLV6700 INB pin 4. U9 OUTB pin 6 sinks only while this voltage is high enough. Q1 source 2 connects OUTB, drain 3 connects BQ CE, and gate 1 connects CHARGE_ALLOW with a 10 kOhm pulldown. CE has a 10 kOhm pullup to VSYS. A hot or shorted sensor releases OUTB and raises CE even if the MCU grants permission. A cold/open sensor is handled by BQ cold protection; these are not two independent complete temperature monitors. Do not disable TS_EN.

Nominal hot entry is approximately **34.51 C**: `0.3945 / 38u - 3300 = 7081.6 ohm`. Log interpolation between the manufacturer's R/T table points estimates cold entry at **3.86 C** and cold recovery at **9.58 C**. Accept this narrower ambient charging window. These temperatures are estimates; neither a nominal threshold nor BQ register labels prove an assembled-cell temperature limit. The series resistor changes the effective BQ hot/cold thresholds, so its programmed 45 C label must not be advertised as a 45 C cell threshold. The earlier 4.53 kOhm candidate was rejected because it left only a 4.58 mV conditional margin at 45 C.

Conditional corner checks use 36.5-39.5 uA TS bias, 25 nA conservative comparator loading, 1% series resistance, full comparator limits, and **assembly acceptance requirements** of R0 >= 26.4616 kOhm, R25 >= 9.70 kOhm and R40 <= 6.00181 kOhm. These bands use the manufacturer's tabulated R/T values with a conservative 3% acceptance band. They are explicit procurement/qualification requirements: the separate R25 and B25/85 tolerances alone do not prove the complete assembled R/T curve. The [calculation script](validate-electrical-contract.py) reproduces every value below.

| Conditional test | Worst voltage | Required threshold | Result |

Film has reserved a 5 x 4.5 x 3.0 mm cell-edge pocket near (15.5, 1.75), but the thermal contact, insulation and wire relief are not engineered or qualified. If R/T acceptance cannot be established, retain the charge hold and review a separately biased temperature monitor; do not expand the charging window by deleting the gate. TI's BQ2518x FAQ supports a constant adapter-mode TS source that cannot be disabled in that mode. BQ25186's own wording around source duty cycling is less explicit; scope the actual chip with CE high, dock insertion/removal, TS_EN changes, watchdog and hot-start conditions. Battery-only bias is not charging-mode evidence. [TI family FAQ](https://e2e.ti.com/support/power-management-group/power-management/f/power-management-forum/1603144/faq-bq2518x-how-does-the-ts-pin-work-for-battery-temperature-monitoring), [BQ timing/thresholds](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq25186.pdf), [TLV thresholds](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv6700.pdf), [Semitec R/T table](https://www.semitec-global.com/uploads/2022/01/P12-13-AT-Thermistor.pdf).

## Dock, user controls and recovery

Choose a **nonmagnetic keyed cradle** for the first build. The motor manufacturer cautions against nearby magnets; a magnetic variant requires field/force qualification. Three lower contacts are **GND / 5 V / GND**, so 180-degree rotation does not reverse polarity. Charger PG supplies insertion status. Dock power is protected 5 V only, with suitable contact plating and wear treatment. The pendant's BAT54WS reverse diode and root-adopted TPD1E10B06DYAR ESD diode do not replace the dock's current limiter. The inherited PESD5V0S1BA was replaced because its 5 V standoff does not cover USB's 5.50 V maximum DC supply. D2 now uses a 5.5 V bidirectional standoff device; C7 is Murata GRM188R61E475KE11D, 4.7 uF /25 V X5R 0603, maximum 1.75 x0.95 x0.95 mm. Its effective capacitance at 5.5 V still needs to meet the charger minimum after bias, temperature, tolerance and aging. [Official USB specification bundle with VBUS Max Limit ECN](https://www.usb.org/document-library/usb-20-specification), [TI D2 ratings and drawing](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tpd1e10b06.pdf), [Murata C7 sheet](https://search.murata.co.jp/Ceramy/image/img/A01X/G101/ENG/GRM188R61E475KE11-01A.pdf).

D2's DYA0002A body is 1.3 x0.85 x0.77 mm maximum, with full lead span1.7 mm; the width excludes up to0.15 mm mold flash per side, so use1.15 mm width for conservative body clearance. TI's example lands are0.67 x0.40 mm at x=+/-0.74 mm, corner radius0.05 mm, with matching paste apertures on a0.1 mm stencil example. Root authored `AuraA04:TI_DYA0002A_TPD1E10B06` through MCP, with a conservative 2.65 x1.65 mm courtyard that also covers the flash allowance. Its 10 V maximum clamp at1 A and14 V maximum at5 A (8/20 us) are below BQ IN's25 V absolute limit; this comparison does not prove PCB overshoot or ESD immunity. Dock TPS2553 has a lower7 V absolute limit and requires separate transient coordination; do not copy the pendant clamp as a proof of dock protection.

Select **TPS2553DBVR** with **ILIM pin 5 wired directly to IN pin 1**. TI explicitly rates this configuration at **50 / 75 / 100 mA min / typical / max DC current limit**, including the device limit spread; no extrapolated large resistor is needed. SOT23-6 pin map is IN1, GND2, EN3, /FAULT4, ILIM5, OUT6. Tie EN to the 5 V input, bypass IN with at least 0.1 uF effective ceramic and additional low-ESR capacitance for ILIM noise, and pull /FAULT up only if used. This configuration guarantees only 50 mA available, so parked low-power charging must fit that minimum and input DPM must reduce charge current when system load rises. A 40 mA target is not a guaranteed rate during transfer or at the lowest limiter corner. This DC limit does not bound the short-circuit transient or discharge of output capacitors. Qualify startup, contact bounce, sustained shorts, reverse contacts, DPM interaction and dock surface temperature. The [dock core contract](dock-contract.json) and [review](dock-review.md) specify proposed USB-C/contact/passive parts and preserve the remaining protection, assembly and qualification gates. [TPS2553 electrical table p.7, configuration p.15 and table p.20](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2553.pdf).

The microphone rail physically goes through SW1. The opposite pole independently reports privacy position; the mic rail powers its central amber LED and both Ioff buffers. With privacy off, the rail is grounded and clock/data cannot power the microphones through MCU pins. Confirm this with injected GPIO levels and measured rail voltage, not just ERC. Firmware must stop and seal capture on privacy transition and distinguish buffered, durable, transferred and processed states.

The face switch handles capture/bookmark and a deliberate pairing gesture. A recessed service switch asserts RESET_N. Hold the face while resetting for signed recovery; SWD remains physically accessible for initial programming. Charger shutdown wakes from dock insertion, not from a disconnected MCU reset switch. The recovery implementation must not erase unsynced notes or accept unsigned firmware. Ring PWM uses unrestricted P0.07; RF-near low-frequency-only module pins remain unused.

## Power, space and release gates

Keep haptics out of active recording and flash maintenance. Motor startup alone is80 mA; a30 mA running figure is not a system peak budget. Main-LDO transient response, rail sag, NAND program current, BLE/Opus simultaneous workload and final cell protection must be measured. A provisional160 mA battery capability is reserved; no runtime claim is supported. At5.50 V and44 mA charging into3.0 V, battery charging alone represents up to110 mW drop loss before system-path/regulator losses. Skin contact and closed-case thermal tests are required.

A35.2 mm disk has about973 mm2 area, only modestly more than the rounded A03 board, while this contract has72 rather than61 PCB parts and adds a10 mm motor. **35.2 mm is not a proven minimum.** Place full real courtyards, manufacturer maximum bodies, acoustic/antenna exclusions and assembly access first. Increase board/body dimensions or simplify if those cannot coexist; never erase courtyards or reduce real bodies to force fit. The upper Y>=9.8 RF reserve and rear26 x21 mm battery volume are provisional mechanical constraints, not footprint clearances.

Before routing: finish exact component footprints/max envelopes and effective capacitor checks, reconcile the actual MCP stack/rules, independently compare every symbol pin to the contract, and run native ERC/netlist parity. Before ordering PCBA: pass real courtyard/max-body/assembly access, copper/paste/drill, antenna and schematic parity checks and supplier DFM with explicit process settings. Before wear or launch: qualify an exact pack/attached sensor, charging/reset/open-short faults, thermal/RF/acoustic/Opus/runtime, recovery and OTA power-loss behavior, and the printed enclosure/dock with actual parts. Digital builds and attractive renders do not supply those measurements.

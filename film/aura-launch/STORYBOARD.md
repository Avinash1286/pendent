The seven shots combine the final A03 Blender photographs with the actual exported GLB. Hero aliases have identical source pixels; separate filenames keep Hyperframes media discovery unambiguous. One paused GSAP timeline owns the edit. The Three.js layer responds to timeline updates and Hyperframes seek events, reconstructing camera and component positions directly from the requested time. It does not use wall-clock time, free-running loops, input, or remote assets.

Assembly poses: assembled at 21.7 s; open by 23.75 s; angled interior hold until 26.15 s; reassembled by 28.1 s; hold to the transition. The same part-name rules and separation factors as the website's assembly explorer are used. The animation is an exploded design study, not a manufacturing procedure or proof of production tolerances.

The original ambient score uses locally synthesized harmonics, sparse bells, and controlled delay. Its harmonic sequence was extended to 44 seconds for the new inside view; the opening and sonic character remain. Picture and music resolve together, without narration or licensed stock footage. The film makes no numerical thickness, capacity, battery-life, reliability, stock, or shipping claims.

The primary line refers to moments the user chooses to capture. Manual capture and the physical mic disconnect remain explicit; the film does not imply always-on recording, complete life capture, or perfect memory.

The product is the center of the story. Use actual Blender geometry, satin reflections, broad photographic lighting, and breathing room. A single, softly lit object deserves more attention than a wall of specifications. Warm ivory headline text stays crisp against graphite; apricot is reserved for a recording moment and a short line. Never use multicolor gradients, fake review stars, invented benchmarks, or crowded feature cards.

At 1920×1080 use 100–144 px statement type, 34–44 px supporting type, and 24–28 px labels. Keep type within a 120 px safe area. Product photographs can run past the image boundary intentionally while text remains entirely legible.

Motion is slow, deliberate, and finite. Gentle product push-ins have constant continuous motion, entrance type resolves with cubic ease-out, and feature state changes use immediate, understandable visual feedback. Final lockup settles and holds. No endless ambient loops, bounce, or gratuitous transitions.

The headline tracking reveal adapts the installed Hyperframes `tracking-in` primitive. The final wordmark settlement and line drawing adapt the installed `titlecard-lockup` primitive. Composition otherwise follows the project's own visual hierarchy.

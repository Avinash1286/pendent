# AURA / pendent

**Be here. Keep the thought.**

An open product-development project for a compact necklace AI note-taking device. The repository brings together the electronics, industrial design, interactive website and launch film.

## Project status

In active development. This is a prototype design, not a manufactured, certified or shipping device. Electrical, acoustic, RF, battery and fit validation must precede fabrication and sale. The website demonstrates the intended companion experience; no live AI service or payment processing is connected.

## Design direction

- 40 × 30 × 12 mm rounded pendant body (design envelope).
- A physical record button, bookmark gesture, visible recording indication and haptic confirmation.
- A physical microphone power disconnect, offline local audio storage and BLE phone transfer.
- Dual digital microphones, a protected rechargeable cell and a magnetic contact charging dock.
- Phone-assisted transcription, summaries, action items and searchable notes as the intended software architecture.

## Publication

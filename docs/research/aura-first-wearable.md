# AURA: the route from Omi's lessons to a first wearable

**The next meaningful milestone is a real spoken note recorded on hardware, recovered after a disconnect, and played back from a durable local copy.** AURA's software foundation has progressed; its custom PCB and powered enclosure are not ready to fabricate and wear.

This plan reviews [Omi at `f42089f53c8010dd971b3702ece05a231c9c0c66`](https://github.com/BasedHardware/omi/tree/f42089f53c8010dd971b3702ece05a231c9c0c66), with AURA's published `0f22273` checkpoint and subsequent storage-controller work distinguished below. It is a source/design review, with no upstream application execution, physical board fabrication or wear trial. The detailed [code review](omi-review.md), [mobile/product review](omi-product-review.md) and [hardware review](omi-hardware-review.md) retain the underlying evidence.

## What to take from Omi

| Lesson from the pinned source | AURA decision and current state |
|---|---|
| Omi prioritizes capture, preservation and retrieval, with reliability ahead of visual novelty. [Product principles](https://github.com/BasedHardware/omi/blob/f42089f53c8010dd971b3702ece05a231c9c0c66/PRODUCT.md#L8-L24), [design guidance](https://github.com/BasedHardware/omi/blob/f42089f53c8010dd971b3702ece05a231c9c0c66/.impeccable.md) | Keep one deliberate capture gesture, visible microphone state and physical privacy control. A premium finish should support a dependable daily ritual. These are A04 requirements; actual control force, indication and comfort are unmeasured. |
| The phone distinguishes download, internet wait, upload and processing, and has a coordinator for recovery. [Sync model](https://github.com/BasedHardware/omi/blob/f42089f53c8010dd971b3702ece05a231c9c0c66/app/lib/models/sync_state.dart#L4-L43), [coordinator](https://github.com/BasedHardware/omi/blob/f42089f53c8010dd971b3702ece05a231c9c0c66/app/lib/services/wals/recording_transfer_coordinator.dart) | Build one foreground phone session with explicit “Saved on pendant,” “Verified on phone” and “Note ready” states. AURA has a desktop receiver and local portal path; a real mobile capture/recovery loop remains missing. |
| The dev kit uses Opus, while consumer firmware targets a different MCU. [Dev-kit configuration](https://github.com/BasedHardware/omi/blob/f42089f53c8010dd971b3702ece05a231c9c0c66/omi/firmware/devkit/src/config.h#L24-L43), [consumer target](https://github.com/BasedHardware/omi/blob/f42089f53c8010dd971b3702ece05a231c9c0c66/omi/firmware/omi/README.md#L5-L13) | Retain AURA's measured-memory Opus port and nRF52840 direction. Run the complete recording workload on hardware next. Omi's nRF5340 consumer binary and XIAO dev-kit wiring are not AURA firmware or pin maps. |
| Selected consumer paths dequeue audio without successful delivery/storage; storage advancement uses notification completion. [Pusher](https://github.com/BasedHardware/omi/blob/f42089f53c8010dd971b3702ece05a231c9c0c66/omi/firmware/omi/src/lib/core/transport.c#L1214-L1254), [notification callback](https://github.com/BasedHardware/omi/blob/f42089f53c8010dd971b3702ece05a231c9c0c66/omi/firmware/omi/src/lib/core/storage.c#L245-L255), [pointer advancement](https://github.com/BasedHardware/omi/blob/f42089f53c8010dd971b3702ece05a231c9c0c66/omi/firmware/omi/src/lib/core/storage.c#L384-L435) | Preserve local audio until the receiver verifies and durably commits the exact capture, followed by authorized release. These static findings identify paths to improve, not observed Omi loss rates. AURA's new release controller/outbox is development work; production audio reclamation is still absent. |
| Source-linked memories and current access checks support trustworthy context. [Memory model](https://github.com/BasedHardware/omi/blob/f42089f53c8010dd971b3702ece05a231c9c0c66/app/lib/backend/schema/memory.dart#L66-L99), [access gateway](https://github.com/BasedHardware/omi/blob/f42089f53c8010dd971b3702ece05a231c9c0c66/backend/models/memory_search_gateway.py) | Keep original audio/transcript, reviewed context and revocable future access. AURA already has local transcription, retained source metadata, Context Packs and read-only MCP. Correction history, complete account lifecycle and phone integration remain work. |

For this first unit, keep Wi-Fi, extra sensors, autonomous tasks and a large memory graph outside the critical path. Reuse Omi code only where it saves integration work and passes AURA's contracts, preserving applicable [license notices](https://github.com/BasedHardware/omi/blob/f42089f53c8010dd971b3702ece05a231c9c0c66/LICENSE).

## What exists today

- **Firmware:** monitored PDM ingress, recorder, real Opus, recoverable NAND journal, command adapter and durable C/Python archive are implemented and exercised by host tests and an ARM integration build. The new owner-bound storage controller and companion release outbox are under development. Their modeled erase path is not a production W25N audio-erase capability, secure enrollment or a complete device application. [Firmware evidence](../../firmware/a04/README.md), [controller source](../../firmware/a04/src/aura_storage.c), [release outbox](../../companion/RELEASE.md).
- **Electronics:** the fresh A04 schematic has 72 candidate references. The circular PCB has **zero footprints, tracks and zones**. Three net mismatches, native ERC findings, duplicate labels, stackup/rule configuration and assembly details remain unresolved. There are no A04 manufacturing Gerbers. [Native status](../../hardware/a04/README.md).
- **Mechanics:** the primary M2 study is **43 mm diameter × 12.2 mm depth**, using the actual 1.6 mm draft PCB thickness. The thinner pack is unselected. The 17 models per variant include polymer parts, separate hardware forms and gauges; they are digitally checked development specimens, not a qualified printed assembly. Steel parts require real metal and insulation. [M2 package](../../enclosure/a04/mechanism-m2/README.md), [steel drawings](../../enclosure/a04/mechanism-m2-manufacturing/README.md).
- **Product:** the existing deployed showcase and film represent A03. They do not demonstrate a working circular A04. No A04 battery, charging dock, assembled radio, physical microphone path or wear trial has been qualified.

## Build and acceptance route

These are proposed first-unit engineering gates, not completed tests or product certification.

| Gate | Concrete next artifact | Acceptance before advancing |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Actual audio/storage bench** | A separate board-specific lab application on a matching nRF52840 development board, with reviewed microphone/W25N wiring, a serialized capture task and diagnostic export. Start on reviewed current-limited external power. The current ARM probe does not operate these peripherals. | Capture 20 spoken notes into real NAND, export, independently decode and play back every accepted note. Power-interrupt a recording and recover the verified prefix with an honest interrupted status. Record encode deadlines, FIFO loss, NAND latency, runtime stack headroom and measured current. |
| **2. One phone recovery loop** | One real phone platform first: enrollment, device inventory, foreground reconnect, durable local audio, playback and explicit context export. Keep background operation an unclaimed capability until measured. | Disconnect and kill the app around transfer/commit, then resume without losing or duplicating verified captures. No network is needed to preserve/play local audio. Releasing one authorized finalized capture preserves all others. Reject a different owner and stale release command. |
| **3. Custom board release** | Correct the fresh MCP-native schematic, freeze exact parts/pack/dock and actual stackup, then place and route. Export the matching Gerber/drill/netlist, purchased BOM, placement, stencil and assembly drawings. | Independently reviewed pin/power/thermal/RF design; complete native ERC/DRC dispositions; maximum-body/tolerance and case checks; supplier CAM/assembly approval; a documented first-article test plan. An empty-board pass or A03 fabrication ZIP cannot satisfy this gate. |
| **4. Unpowered fit and controls** | Print the process coupon, gauges and M2 development specimens; fit measured hardware, insulating interfaces and a representative inert pack. Record the assembled mass and dimensions. | Verify the complete assembly sequence, microphone openings, antenna space, service access, switch endpoints/force, thread retention and attachment. Confirm the pack bears no switch or clamping load. Revise geometry from measurements before claiming a thin final size. |
| **5. Powered first article, then personal wear** | Assemble the reviewed PCB; inspect and bring up rails/SWD/audio/storage before the matched battery/NTC/dock. Complete the actual firmware's privacy, radio and recovery integration. | Measure charging limits and faults against the exact approved component limits, enclosure temperatures, RF with the actual chain/body, acoustic quality and workload runtime. After bench/attachment/insulation acceptance, progress through a short supervised wear trial, two hours, then an eight-hour target with a stated recording workload. Record discomfort, heat, snagging and missed captures; charging during engineering evaluation stays off body. |

The next software work should make **Gate 1 executable**, then connect Gate 2 to the same recording contract. Further host tests are useful when they cover a newly identified defect; they cannot supply microphone timing, real flash behavior, usable phone recovery or comfort evidence. Prepare Gate 4 in parallel and let its measurements constrain the board and enclosure together.

Omi's [XIAO assembly guide](https://github.com/BasedHardware/omi/blob/f42089f53c8010dd971b3702ece05a231c9c0c66/docs/doc/assembly/Build_the_device.mdx) is an alternative learning build if a separate Omi-compatible trial is desired. It has its own firmware, case, power wiring and phone path. It does not validate AURA's independent microphone cutoff, local journal, battery or mechanics; introducing that second port is unnecessary for the primary A04 bench milestone.

For each physical trial, retain the board/firmware/part revisions, setup photograph, workload, measurements, source audio, interruption point and pass/fail disposition in a private first-unit log. Publish sanitized reports with the eventual release; keep personal recordings and enrollment keys private.

Required KiCad corrections remain blocked by recorded tool declines without a supplied reason. The rejected operations have not been retried through another mechanism or bypassed. This must be resolved before the custom PCB fabrication gate can close; the independent firmware and unpowered fit work can continue.

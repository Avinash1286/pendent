## Intent

A premium launch film for the AURA A03 capsule: a battery-powered note-taking pendant with a glossy black full-face recording paddle, satin silver frame, and fine chain. The emotional story moves from life's passing moments to keeping what matters, personal control, and new possibilities through context. The exact primary line is “Capture the context of your life.” Existing product photography, measured motion, original music, and the long beauty hold remain central. The user explicitly delegates all decisions and requests a finished video using Hyperframes.

## Customizations

- 44 seconds, 1920×1080, 30 fps, MP4/H.264 with AAC stereo audio.
- Seekable GSAP motion driven entirely by Hyperframes.
- Eight-second Three.js inside view: assembled object → separated layers → interior hold → assembled object. The user's added request for an inside look and assembly animation is part of this revision.
- No voiceover: the product, measured typography, and original score lead the film.
- Graphite, warm ivory, black gloss, satin silver, and a small apricot accent.

## Notes

All shown features are design intent for a development concept. No shipping date, live checkout, certification, measured battery life, or demonstrated AI performance is claimed. The phone notes screen is an explicitly labeled interface concept. No user decision or approval checkpoint is required: the user expressly asked the agent to choose and finish the entire deliverable.

Context refers to the thoughts and moments a person chooses to capture. “Press to capture,” the physical mic disconnect, and “Capture starts with you” make that choice explicit. The film does not suggest always-on recording or complete capture of someone's life.
